Descripción detallada

Single entry in a PerfProfile.

In DNN_PROFILE_DETAILED mode, label is "layer_name (type)" and count is 1. In DNN_PROFILE_SUMMARY mode, label is the layer type and count is the number of layers of that type that contributed to timeMs.
