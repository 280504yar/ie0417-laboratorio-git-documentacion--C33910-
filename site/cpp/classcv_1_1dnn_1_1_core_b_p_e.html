Descripción detallada

Core Byte Pair Encoding (BPE) engine (mergeable-ranks model).

Encodes and decodes tokens at the byte level (UTF-8 input is split to bytes), with optional support for special tokens that are matched by a separate regex.

The implementation follows the structure of OpenAI’s tiktoken encoders.

Encode text with optional special tokens.

Scans text for allowed special tokens, emits them as single ids, and BPE-encodes the intervening ordinary segments.

Parámetros
textUTF-8 input.
allowedSpecialSet of literal special-token strings that may appear and be emitted.
Devuelve
Pair (tokens, last_piece_token_len) where:
  • tokens is the full token sequence,
  • last_piece_token_len is the number of tokens produced by the final ordinary segment (0 if the text ended with a special token).
