template<typename T>
class cv::LINEMOD< T >

Given a depth image, compute the normals as detailed in the LINEMOD paper Gradient Response Maps for Real-Time Detection of Texture-Less Objects by S. Hinterstoisser, C. Cagniart, S. Ilic, P. Sturm, N. Navab, P. Fua, and V. Lepetit
