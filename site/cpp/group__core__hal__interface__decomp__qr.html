Descripción detallada

Performs QR decomposition of ( ) matrix and solves matrix equation .

Parámetros
src1pointer to input matrix stored in row major order. After finish of work src1 contains upper triangular matrix . Lower triangle of src1 will be filled with vectors of elementary reflectors. See [VandLec] and Lapack's DGEQRF documentation for details.
src1_stepnumber of bytes between two consequent rows of matrix .
mnumber fo rows in matrix .
nnumber of columns in matrix .
knumber of right-hand vectors in matrix .
src2pointer to matrix which is the right-hand side of system . stored in row major order. If src2 is null pointer only QR decomposition will be performed. Otherwise system will be solved and src1 will be used as temporary buffer, so after finish of work src2 contains solution of system .
src2_stepnumber of bytes between two consequent rows of matrix .
dstpointer to continiuos array for scalar factors of elementary reflectors. See [VandLec] for details.
infoindicates success of decomposition. If *info is zero decomposition failed.
