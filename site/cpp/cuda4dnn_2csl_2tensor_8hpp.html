Tensor is implicitly convertible to TensorSpan and TensorView TensorSpan is implicitly convertible to TensorView

Concepts and template parameter naming convention:

  • "MutableTensorType" can refer to a Tensor or TensorSpan
  • "ImmutableTensorType" can refer to a Tensor, TensorSpan or TensorView
  • "TensorType" can refer to a Tensor, TensorSpan or TensorView

"ImmutableTensorType" is used when the tensor data might be used. "TensorType" is used when only meta-information such as the size or shape is required, i.e. the data won't be touched
