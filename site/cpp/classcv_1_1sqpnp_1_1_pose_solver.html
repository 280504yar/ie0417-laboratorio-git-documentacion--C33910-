Finds the possible poses of a camera given a set of 3D points and their corresponding 2D image projections. The poses are sorted by lowest squared error (which corresponds to lowest reprojection error).

Parámetros
objectPointsArray or vector of 3 or more 3D points defined in object coordinates. 1xN/Nx1 3-channel (float or double) where N is the number of points.
imagePointsArray or vector of corresponding 2D points, 1xN/Nx1 2-channel.
rvecThe output rotation solutions (up to 18 3x1 rotation vectors)
tvecThe output translation solutions (up to 18 3x1 vectors)
