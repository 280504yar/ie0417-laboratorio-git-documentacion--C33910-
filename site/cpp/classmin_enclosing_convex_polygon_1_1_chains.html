Descripción detallada

Class for building the one-sided and h-sided chains and calculation the minimum enclosing polygon based on those chains.

Parámetros
ngonInput polygon
single_sidesMatrix of the best one-sided chain for each combination of two sides of the ngon
middle_sidesMatrix of the middle side of each h-sided chain for all relevant h and each combination of two sides of the ngon
intersectionsAn instance of FlushIntersections to store already calculated flush intersections
balanced_intersAn instance of BalancedIntersections to store already calculated balanced intersections
