Descripción detallada

Container for the information about a balanced side between two points.

Parámetros
piFirst intersection point
pjSecond intersection point
extra_areaThe area that would be added to the kgon compared to the ngon
flushFlush is a special case of balanced. If the balanced side is also flush, this indicates which of the sides of the ngon it is flush with. The default for not flush sides is -1.
positionIs the balanced side a valid solution?
doneSet to true once calculated to avoid redundant calculations
