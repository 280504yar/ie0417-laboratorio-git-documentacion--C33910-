Descripción detallada

non-copyable cuBLAS smart handle

UniqueHandle is a smart non-sharable wrapper for cuBLAS handle which ensures that the handle is destroyed after use. The handle must always be associated with a non-default stream. The stream must be specified during construction.

Refer to stream API for more information for the choice of forcing non-default streams.
