Descripción detallada

Performs Cholesky decomposition of matrix and solves matrix equation .

Parámetros
src1pointer to input matrix stored in row major order. After finish of work src1 contains lower triangular matrix .
src1_stepnumber of bytes between two consequent rows of matrix .
msize of square matrix .
src2pointer to matrix which is the right-hand side of system . B stored in row major order. If src2 is null pointer only Cholesky decomposition will be performed. After finish of work src2 contains solution of system .
src2_stepnumber of bytes between two consequent rows of matrix .
nnumber of right-hand vectors in matrix .
infoindicates success of decomposition. If *info is false decomposition failed.
